 # ⚡ Hydroelectric Power Generator

### Renewable Energy Generation Using Flowing Water

## 1. Project Overview
This project demonstrates how electrical energy can be generated using the energy of flowing water. It aims to create a small-scale hydroelectric power generation model.

## 2. Problem Statement
Increasing electricity demand and environmental pollution create a need for affordable and clean renewable energy sources.

## 3. Proposed Solution
A small hydroelectric generator that converts the energy of flowing water into electrical energy using a turbine and generator.

## 4. Objectives
- Understand hydroelectric power generation.
- Generate electricity using flowing water.
- Demonstrate renewable energy technology.
- Develop an affordable working model.

## 5. Components Required
- DC motor (used as generator)
- Water turbine
- Water pump or flowing water source
- Connecting wires
- LED
- Water container
- Supporting structure

## 6. Working Principle
1. Water flows towards the turbine.
2. Flowing water rotates the turbine.
3. The turbine drives the DC motor.
4. The motor converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
5. The generated electricity can power an LED.

## 7. Applications
- Small-scale electricity generation
- Renewable energy demonstrations
- Educational science projects
- Micro-hydropower systems

## 8. Future Improvements
- Measure voltage and current.
- Add an Arduino monitoring system.
- Display power generation data.
- Improve turbine efficiency.
