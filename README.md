 # Hardware Setup – Hydroelectric Power Generator

## 1. Components Required

| S.No | Component | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dynamo | Generates electrical energy |
| 2 | Water Pump | Supplies flowing water |
| 3 | Metal Turbine | Converts water flow into rotational motion |
| 4 | Connecting Wires | Electrical connections |
| 5 | Supporting Structure | Holds the components |

## 2. Working Principle

1. The water pump supplies water.
2. Water flows towards the metal turbine.
3. Flowing water rotates the turbine blades.
4. The turbine transfers rotational motion to the dynamo.
5. The dynamo generates electrical output.

## 3. Hardware Connection

Water Pump → Water Flow → Metal Turbine → Dynamo → Electrical Output

## 5. Safety Precautions

- Keep electrical connections protected from water.
- Use suitable low-voltage components.
- Check connections before testing.
- Never handle electrical connections with wet hands.
