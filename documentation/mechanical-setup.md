# Mechanical Setup – Hydroelectric Power Generator

## 1. Mechanical Components
- Metal water turbine
- 12V dynamo
- Belt
- Turbine pulley
- Dynamo pulley
- Supporting frame

## 2. Belt Drive Mechanism
The turbine and dynamo are connected using a belt drive mechanism.

When flowing water rotates the turbine, the belt transfers rotational motion to the dynamo.

## 3. Working Process
1. Water rotates the metal turbine.
2. The turbine shaft rotates its pulley.
3. The belt transfers rotational motion.
4. The dynamo shaft rotates.
5. The dynamo generates electrical output.

## 4. Testing
- Check turbine rotation.
- Check belt alignment.
- Check belt tension.
- Observe dynamo rotation.

Mechanical assembly and testing are in progress.
