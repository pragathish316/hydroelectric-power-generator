Project Results & Analysis

Hydroelectric power generation - software demonstration

System Configuration

Generator: 12V-rated dynamo

Turbine: Metal turbine

Transmission: Belt drive

Water source: Water pump

Analysis

The simulation demonstrates a simple relationship between water-flow input and estimated voltage. Actual output depends on turbine speed, dynamo characteristics and electrical load.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the conversion of water energy into mechanical energy and then electrical energy. The software modules help explain the system without requiring connected sensors.
