HydroPower Simulator

Software-based demonstration of a hydroelectric power generation system.

Project Objective

To explain how flowing water can rotate a turbine and how a dynamo converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.

Hardware Setup

Water pump, metal turbine, belt-drive mechanism and a 12V-rated dynamo.

Software Modules

  • Water Flow Control
  • Energy Calculator
  • Power Monitoring Simulation

Working Principle

Water flow rotates the turbine. The belt transfers rotation to the dynamo, which generates electrical energy.
