HydroPower - User Guide

Welcome to the HydroPower Generator Software.

1. Dashboard

Open the dashboard to access the different project modules.

2. Water Flow Simulator

Adjust the water flow slider to observe illustrative changes in turbine speed and simulated voltage.

3. Energy Calculator

Enter voltage, current and operating time to calculate electrical power and energy.

4. Data Storage

Save and review calculation records in your current browser.

Saved records are stored locally on your device.

5. Performance and Analysis

Explore sample simulation readings and understand how water flow can affect generator output.

Important Note

This software is an educational simulation. It does not receive live readings from the physical generator.
