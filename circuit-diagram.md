## 2. Working Principle

1. The water pump supplies flowing water.
2. Water rotates the metal turbine.
3. The turbine pulley drives the belt.
4. The belt rotates the dynamo.
5. The dynamo converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
6. The generated voltage is measured using a multimeter.

## 3. Electrical Testing

- Check dynamo output.
- Measure voltage using a multimeter.
- Record actual voltage readings.
- Verify output polarity and type before connecting a load.

## 4. Safety

- Keep electrical components dry.
- Avoid short circuits.
- Do not connect an LED directly without checking voltage and using suitable protection.
