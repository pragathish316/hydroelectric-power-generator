Technical Specifications

Hydroelectric Power Generation Project

System Components

ComponentSpecification
Water SourceWater pump
TurbineMetal turbine
TransmissionBelt drive
Generator12V-rated dynamo

Energy Conversion

Hydraulic Energy → Mechanical Energy → Electrical Energy

Working Principle

Water from the pump flows towards the metal turbine. The turbine rotates and transfers mechanical motion through a belt to the dynamo. The dynamo converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.

Important Technical Note

12V is the dynamo's rated voltage, not a guaranteed constant output. Actual voltage and power depend on rotational speed and electrical load.
