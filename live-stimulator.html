Working Principle

Water flow rotates the turbine. The turbine drives the dynamo, which converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.

Electrical Power = Voltage × Current

Electrical Energy = Power × Time

Simulation Information

This is an educational software simulation. The readings are calculated using illustrative assumptions and are not actual dynamo measurements. Actual generator output depends on RPM, load and generator characteristics.
