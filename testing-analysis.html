Analysis

In this illustrative model, increasing water flow increases turbine speed and simulated voltage.

Actual output depends on turbine speed, generator characteristics and electrical load.

Conclusion

The software model helps explain the working principle and estimate how changing water flow can affect output. Actual testing requires measured electrical readings.
